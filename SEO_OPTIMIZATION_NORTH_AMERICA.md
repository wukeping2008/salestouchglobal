## 🎯 Content Optimization Strategy

### Keyword Density Guidelines
- **Title tags**: 50-60 characters, include primary keyword
- **Meta descriptions**: 150-160 characters, compelling + keywords
- **H1 tags**: 1 per page, primary keyword
- **H2-H6 tags**: Hierarchical structure, include variations
- **Paragraph length**: 100-150 words, keyword density 1-2%
- **Internal linking**: 3-5 relevant links per page
- **External linking**: 2-3 high-authority sources per page

### Content Structure
1. **Hero Section** (Above fold)
   - Compelling headline with primary keyword
   - Value proposition
   - Primary CTA with keyword in button text
   - Trust signals (logos, stats)

2. **Features Section**
   - Benefit-driven headings
   - Use keyword variations
   - Include social proof and case studies

3. **Social Proof Section**
   - Customer testimonials with names and photos
   - Case study summaries with ROI numbers
   - "Trusted by" logos

4. **FAQ Section** (SEO Goldmine)
   - Question-answer format targeting long-tail keywords
   - Natural language matching user queries
   - Include schema markup

5. **Footer**
   - Sitemap link
   - Privacy policy
   - Terms of service
   - Contact information with NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
