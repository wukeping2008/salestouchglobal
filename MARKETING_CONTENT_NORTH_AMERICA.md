❌ Expecting 100% adoption and immediate results
✅ Set realistic 90-day adoption goals
✅ Track and celebrate small wins early on

❌ Applying the same coaching strategy to all salespeople
✅ Tailor coaching approaches based on role (SDR, AE, AM, Manager)
✅ Customize based on experience level
✅ Adapt to different verticals and deal sizes

Mistake #4: Ignoring the human element
❌ Relying solely on AI feedback without human coaching oversight
✅ Pair AI insights with manager reviews
✅ Encourage peer learning and sharing
✅ Schedule regular team retrospectives

❌ Tracking vanity metrics instead of outcomes
✅ Focus on close rates, deal velocity, revenue growth
✅ Establish baseline measurements before implementation
✅ Review and adjust metrics quarterly

Mistake #6: Poor change management
❌ Rolling out new tools without clear communication
✅ Create a rollout plan with phased implementation
✅ Communicate benefits and address concerns upfront
✅ Provide ongoing support and resources

Mistake #7: Underestimating training needs
❌ Assuming AI tools are intuitive without guidance
✅ Invest in comprehensive training programs
✅ Create quick-start guides and video tutorials
✅ Assign AI champions to help peers
